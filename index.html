We're ASHRAE
at RIT Dubai.

We talk HVAC, energy and buildings that don't waste the planet.

So what is ASHRAE?

The American Society of Heating, Refrigerating and Air-Conditioning Engineers. A global society that started in New York in 1894 and now has members in 130+ countries.

They write the standards that decide how buildings breathe, stay comfortable and use energy. Pretty much every building you've been in today followed one.

Standards you'll hear about

90.1

Energy. How efficient a building has to be.

62.1

Ventilation. How much fresh air people need inside.

55

Thermal comfort. Why the room feels just right, or doesn't.

Handbook

The bible. Four volumes every HVAC engineer reaches for.
